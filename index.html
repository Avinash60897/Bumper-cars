❔ How to Play

Goal: knock the ball into the red goal. First to the target score wins.
Teams: each side has 1 or 2 karts. Pick a Goalkeeper or a Striker for each slot. The goalkeeper stays inside its penalty box.
Drive: WASD or arrows. On a phone, drag on the field to steer.
Boost: Space (P2: Enter) · Drift: hold Shift while turning, then let go for a turbo.
Power-ups: drive over the glowing orbs, then press E (P2: /).
